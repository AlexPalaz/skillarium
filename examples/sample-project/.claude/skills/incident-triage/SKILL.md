# Incident Triage

Stabilize first, diagnose second.

## First five minutes

1. **Impact:** who is affected, and since when?
2. **Change:** what was deployed or reconfigured recently?
3. **Mitigate:** roll back, fail over, or shed load.

## Communication

Post a status update every 30 minutes, even when there is nothing new.
